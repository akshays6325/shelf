# Shelf privacy policy

Shelf is a personal app store run by Akshay Singh for himself, friends and family.

**What Shelf collects when you sign in with Google:** your Google account ID, name, email address and
profile photo. Nothing else from your Google account is requested.

**What you add:** star ratings and reviews you write, plus the app version and phone model you posted
from, so bugs can be reproduced.

**Who sees it:** your name, photo, ratings and reviews are visible to other Shelf users. Your email,
phone model and app version are only visible to the developer.

**Where it's stored:** on the Shelf review server (Render) and its database (Turso). It is not sold,
shared or used for ads.

**Deleting it:** Shelf → profile → *Delete account* removes your profile and all your reviews
immediately. You can also sign out at any time.
